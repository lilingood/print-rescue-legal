04 — Printer Disclaimer

Results vary — and that's expected

Print Rescue's compensation printing technology is designed to work with AirPrint-compatible inkjet printers. However, print quality improvement is not guaranteed and depends on several factors beyond our control:

  • The severity, pattern, and number of clogged nozzles in your printer.
  • The specific printer model, firmware version, and driver behavior.
  • Paper type, ink type, and environmental conditions.
  • Accuracy of calibration performed by the user.

Important: Print Rescue performs standard AirPrint print jobs via iOS. It does not physically clean, repair, or alter your printer hardware in any way. The App does not assume responsibility for any hardware malfunction, ink consumption, or paper waste that occurs during normal use.

We recommend completing a calibration session before your first compensation print to maximize accuracy. Results shown in promotional materials reflect testing on standard AirPrint-compatible inkjet devices with specific nozzle fault patterns; your results may differ.
