01 — Getting Started

Your printer isn't dead — let's prove it

What Print Rescue does: When an inkjet printer develops clogged nozzles, it leaves blank horizontal streaks across the page. Print Rescue uses a smart two-pass printing method to fill those streaks in — no hardware repair, no replacement parts.

Before your first recovery print, complete a one-time calibration so the app learns exactly where the streaks appear on your specific printer.

  1. Print a diagnostic test page. Tap Diagnostic in the app to send a test pattern to your printer. This reveals which nozzle groups are blocked and where the blank zones fall on the page.

  2. Calibrate. Either photograph the test page with your camera for automatic detection, or tap the white-band positions manually on screen. The app saves your printer's profile for all future jobs.

  3. Start a recovery print. Go to Home, import the document you want to print (PDF, image, or Word file), and tap Print. The app handles the rest — automatically.

Calibration only needs to be done once per printer, or whenever the clog pattern changes significantly.

02 — How It Works

Smart Recovery Printing, explained simply

Print Rescue uses a two-pass approach to restore full-coverage output from a partially clogged printer:

  • Pass 1 — Standard Print. Your document is printed normally. Blocked nozzles leave white streaks in predictable positions across the page.
  • Pass 2 — Recovery Print. You feed the same sheet back into the printer. The app sends a precisely offset version of your document, calculated so that the healthy nozzles now cover exactly the zones that were missed in Pass 1.

The result: a complete, streak-free page — without touching the print head, without expensive cleaning cycles, and without any hardware repair.

Works with any AirPrint-compatible inkjet printer. Tested extensively with Epson EcoTank and Expression series. Results vary by the severity and pattern of blockage.

04 — Troubleshooting

Something not working?

The app can't find my printer.
Make sure your iPhone and printer are on the same Wi-Fi network. AirPrint requires both devices to be on the same local network. If your printer was recently added, try toggling Wi-Fi off and on again on your iPhone.

The recovery print still shows streaks.
Re-run the calibration to make sure the white-band positions are mapped correctly for your current blockage pattern. Small shifts in the clog can affect alignment. For very severe or irregularly distributed blockages, multiple recovery attempts may be needed.

The second pass is misaligned — content shifted up or down.
This usually means the paper was not fed back in the same orientation, or the printer shifted the starting point. Ensure the sheet is placed in the tray in the same direction as during the first pass. Re-calibrate if the issue persists.

The camera calibration failed to detect bands.
Photograph the test page on a flat, well-lit surface without shadows or glare. Ensure the entire page is visible within the camera frame. If detection remains unreliable, use the manual tap-calibration mode instead — it requires no camera access.

My subscription didn't restore after reinstalling.
Tap Restore Purchases inside the app (Settings → Subscription). Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

If your issue isn't listed here, please contact us directly — we're happy to help troubleshoot your specific printer model and situation.

05 — Subscriptions & Purchases

Plans, billing, and cancellation

Print Rescue offers a free tier for basic use, and optional premium plans — monthly, annual, and lifetime — that unlock unlimited recovery printing and advanced calibration modes.

  • All purchases are processed by Apple via the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
  • To cancel or manage your subscription, go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.
  • To restore a purchase after reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases inside the app.
  • Refund requests are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to submit a refund request.

Lifetime purchase is a one-time payment for permanent access to all current premium features on the purchasing device and any device signed in to the same Apple ID.
