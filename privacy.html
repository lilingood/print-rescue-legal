01 — Overview

Your privacy is our default

Short version: Print Rescue does not collect, store, or share your personal data. Everything happens on your device. We have no servers that receive your documents, photos, or printer information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Print Rescue ("the App", "we", "our") handles when you use the application on your iPhone or iPad. We are committed to minimal data practices consistent with Apple's App Store guidelines and global privacy standards.

By using Print Rescue, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

03 — Camera Access

Camera — used for calibration only

Print Rescue may request access to your device camera for the Automatic Calibration feature. When you photograph a printed test page, the App uses Apple's Vision framework to detect white-band positions and calculate compensation offsets.

  • Camera images are processed entirely on-device using the Vision framework.
  • No photo is saved to your Camera Roll unless you explicitly choose to save it.
  • No image data is transmitted off your device under any circumstances.
  • You may use the manual tap-calibration mode instead; camera access is never required.

You can revoke camera permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

04 — In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Payments are handled entirely by Apple

Print Rescue offers optional premium access via monthly, annual, and lifetime plans. All payment processing is managed exclusively by Apple through StoreKit 2.

We never see your payment card details. Apple processes and encrypts all financial transactions. Your purchase history is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Print Rescue only receives a confirmation token from Apple indicating whether a valid purchase or active subscription exists — nothing more. No financial data reaches our systems.

To manage or cancel subscriptions, go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your device.

05 — Local Data Storage

What stays on your device

The App saves the following data locally in your device's sandboxed storage to preserve your settings between sessions:

  • Calibration profiles — nozzle group markings, white-band count, anchor coordinates (in mm), and strip height.
  • Printer configuration — selected printer model preferences.
  • App preferences — language and display settings.

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the App permanently removes all locally stored data.

Print Rescue does not use iCloud sync for calibration data. Your profiles are personal to the device they were created on.

06 — Third Parties

We share nothing with third parties

Print Rescue does not sell, rent, license, or share any user data with third parties for advertising, analytics, data brokerage, or any other purpose.

The App contains no embedded SDKs from social media platforms, ad networks, or tracking providers.

The App communicates with two external endpoints only:

  • Your AirPrint printer — on your local Wi-Fi network, to send print jobs.
  • Apple's StoreKit servers — to verify subscription status.

Both communications are initiated solely at your request and carry no personal data about you.

07 — Children's Privacy

Not directed at children

Print Rescue is a productivity utility designed for general audiences. It is not directed at children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

08 — Security

Security by design

Because Print Rescue stores no personal data on external servers, the attack surface for data breaches is minimal. All on-device data is protected by iOS's built-in sandboxing and device encryption (protected by your passcode / Face ID / Touch ID).
